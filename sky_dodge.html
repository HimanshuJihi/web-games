Play Sky Dodge Online - Free HTML5 Arcade Survival Game

Sky Dodge is a thrilling survival game where you control a small spaceship and try to stay alive while meteors and hazards fall from the sky. The longer you survive, the more points you earn and the faster the challenge becomes.

How to Play Sky Dodge?

Move left or right using keyboard arrows, touch input, or mouse movement to dodge incoming meteors. Avoid collisions, collect bonus stars, and keep your three lives alive for as long as possible.

  • Move constantly: Stay centered and react quickly to sudden meteor drops.
  • Avoid collisions: Every hit takes away a life.
  • Collect stars: Bonus stars increase your score.
  • Survive longer: Longer survival times mean bigger rewards and better rankings.
