Play Star Catcher Online - Free HTML5 Arcade Game

Star Catcher is a fast and colorful arcade game where you move a rocket ship across the screen and catch glowing stars while avoiding dangerous bombs. The challenge gets more intense as time passes, making it perfect for short bursts of fun on both desktop and mobile.

How to Play Star Catcher?

Use your mouse, touch controls, or arrow keys to move the ship left and right. Catch stars for points, collect hearts for extra lives, and avoid the dark bombs that can end your run.

  • Catch stars: Earn 10 points for every successful catch.
  • Avoid bombs: Each bomb costs one life, so stay sharp.
  • Collect hearts: These give you an extra life and help keep your streak alive.
  • Beat your best: Try to finish with the highest score before the timer ends.
